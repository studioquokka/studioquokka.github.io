Duplicate Fighter Summons · 신화 만능 설계도

A duplicate of a fighter you already own = a blueprint for that fighter (each fighter has a cap). A duplicate summon of a fighter that has reached its cap (fully upgraded) = an upgrade kit + the reward below (fighters chosen from selection boxes or with 신화 만능 설계도 are excluded). Fully upgraded Legendary: if you still have a Legendary that is not fully upgraded, 2 Legendary blueprint selection boxes; if all Legendaries are fully upgraded, only the kit. Fully upgraded Mythic: if you still have a Mythic that is not fully upgraded, 신화 만능 설계도 x5. 신화 만능 설계도 x10 = choose 1 Mythic that is not fully upgraded (the fighter itself if you do not own it, or 1 blueprint if you do). On Mythic nodes, 2 universal blueprints count as 1 blueprint.
