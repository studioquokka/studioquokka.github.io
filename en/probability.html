Dungeon Part Shards

Clearing a dungeon rewards part shard drops (거대 전함 작전 2 drop(s) · 무리 섬멸 작전 1 drop(s) · 장갑 요새 작전 2 drop(s)). 무리 섬멸 작전 rewards equipment when you clear it by defeating the Legion Queen, who appears after 200 kills, within the time limit. Each drop is rolled separately with the probabilities below.

Max progress tier (set by your best stage) = chapters cleared + 2, +1 from the 3rd stage of the current chapter (up to 17). Tiers 0-2 = Common, 3-5 = Magic, 6-8 = Rare, 9-11 = Epic, 12-14 = Legendary, 15-17 = Mythic. One drop = part shards of the rolled tier's grade (Common 1 / Magic 2 / Rare 4 / Epic 8 / Legendary 15 / Mythic 30). Example: after clearing Chapter 12, Stages 1-2 of Chapter 13 = 12 + 2 = 14; from Stage 3 of Chapter 13 = 12 + 2 + 1 = 15. "Chapter N cleared" in the tables below means Stage 1 of Chapter N+1.

Base tier = max progress tier + (dungeon Lv - 1) ÷ 4 (remainder dropped, up to 17). Normal range = base tier - 3 to base tier (weight of a tier = 0.55^(range max - tier), Mythic tiers x4; per-grade probabilities are in the tables below). From dungeon Lv.11, a Mythic branch with probability min(50%, 5% x (Lv - 10)) drops from the Mythic range instead of the normal range. Mythic range = 15 to min(17, max(15 + (Lv - 11) ÷ 5, one tier above the normal range max)) - the Mythic branch never gives a tier lower than the normal range. The Mythic grade probability below is the actual probability including Mythic tiers from the normal range, and is the same value as "Mythic grade probability" in the in-game dungeon details.
