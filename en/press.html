About the game

The year is 2187. The Void Legion, a swarm where machine and flesh have grown into one, has taken humanity's skies. The last fighter of the Orbital Defense Squadron crosses planets and moons to win the front lines back, one sector at a time.

  • Your squadron fights while you rest - Your fighter flies, dodges and fires on its own. Close the app and the squadron holds the line for up to 8 hours, gathering supplies for your return.
  • Take the stick any time - Touch the screen to take control at once. A tiny hitbox lets you thread through bullet curtains.
  • Around 70 bosses - Each boss changes form and its bullet patterns change with it. Every big attack is telegraphed: read it, dodge it, strike back.
  • Build your own squadron - Mix 7 weapon families into your loadout, collect fighters and upgrade them part by part, and unleash each pilot's signature ultimate.
