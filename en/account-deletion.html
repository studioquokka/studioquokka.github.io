Ace in the Void Account and Data Deletion Request

Developer: Studio Quokka · App: "Ace in the Void" (com.studioquokka.ace)

This page explains how to ask for your game account and related data to be deleted. You can make a request even if you have already uninstalled the game.

1. Delete in the game

  1. In the game, tap [Etc].
  2. Tap [Settings] → [Delete Data].
  3. Read the notice, then tap [Continue] and hold [Hold to Delete] for 2 seconds.

2. Request on the web (by email)

If you have uninstalled the game or cannot open it, please send us an email.

  • To: studioquokka.support@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account deletion request - Ace in the Void
  • Body: your game ID (shown at the bottom of the [Etc] screen, or in the subject of a support email). If you do not know it, please include your device model and the date you last played.

After confirming it is you, we will process the request within 30 days and tell you the result by email.

3. Data we delete

  • Your game account (player ID) and your progress, leaderboard records and mailbox on the game server
  • Play analytics data (if you agreed to it)
  • Support emails (after the reply is complete)

4. Data we keep as required by law

  • Payment records: 5 years from the transaction date
  • Abuse records: 1 year from the date recorded

Data we keep is used only for the purposes above and is deleted when the period ends.

5. Please note

  • After deletion, your progress, virtual items and purchased items cannot be restored.
  • If you have unused paid currency, please ask whether a refund is possible before deleting.
  • You can unlink data held separately by ad companies by resetting or deleting your device's advertising ID.
