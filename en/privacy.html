Ace in the Void Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28
Applies to: the mobile game "Ace in the Void" for Android (package name com.studioquokka.ace) and iOS
Developer: Studio Quokka ("we", "the developer")

Summary: your game progress is stored on your device by default. To connect to the game server, process in-app purchases and show ads, the minimum data described below is shared with the companies we work with (Unity, Google, Apple). Personalized ads and play analytics are your choice, and you can change that choice at any time.

1. Data we collect and why

We do not ask for or read your name, phone number, address, precise location, contacts or photos. There is no sign-up; the game uses an anonymous account made of a random number.

  • Game account (anonymous): on first launch the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services and creates a random player ID. Your IP address and device information (operating system, device model, language, app version) are sent at that time. Purpose: checking server time, showing announcements, preventing abuse and (when these features are on) server saves, leaderboards and the mailbox.
  • Game progress: stored encrypted on your device. If you use server saves, the same record is also stored on the game server. Purpose: providing the game and letting you continue on a new device.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your card number or other payment method details. We receive the order number, product, purchase time and receipt to check that a purchase is genuine, deliver the item and handle refunds.
  • Advertising data: to show rewarded ads, your advertising ID (Android advertising ID; iOS IDFA only if you allow it), IP address, device information and records of ads you view and tap are sent to ad companies. Purpose: showing ads, granting ad rewards, preventing ad fraud and (with your consent) personalized ads.
  • Consent records: your ad and analytics choices, the time you made them and the country set on your device are stored on your device. Purpose: honoring and demonstrating your choices.
  • Play analytics (optional): only if you agree to "Play analytics" in the settings may we send in-game event records (stage progress, menu use, game ID). If you do not agree, nothing is sent (default = not agreed).
  • Abuse records: when abuse such as leaderboard manipulation, forged payments, save tampering or speed hacks is detected, the event and its time are recorded on the game server. Purpose: keeping the game fair.
  • Support email: if you email us through [Customer Support], we receive your email address, your message and the game ID in the subject line. Purpose: replying to you.
  • Data kept only on your device: game settings (sound, control sensitivity and similar) and a local play log (at most 512 KB, older entries are deleted automatically). Notifications are scheduled on the device only; no push token is created.

To stop the save file from being opened on another device, a device identifier is used only on the device to create the encryption key. It is never stored or sent.

2. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process personal data on the following legal bases: providing the game, payments and the anonymous account to perform our contract with you; abuse prevention, security and ad-fraud prevention for our legitimate interests; personalized ads and play analytics with your consent; and keeping payment records to comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing, processors and international transfers

We do not sell your personal data. To run the game we entrust data to, or share data with, the companies below. Their servers are outside the Republic of Korea (mainly in the United States), and data is sent over the internet in encrypted form while you use the app.

  • Unity Technologies Inc. (USA) - Unity Gaming Services (anonymous sign-in, game server, server saves, remote config): game ID, player ID, IP address, device information, game progress, abuse records. Retention: section 6.
  • Google LLC (USA) - Google Play billing, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP, the ad consent form), Google Mobile Ads SDK: order data, advertising ID, IP address, device information, consent choices.
  • Apple Inc. (USA) - App Store payments and the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choice: order data, your ATT choice.
  • Ad companies: the list in section 4.

You may refuse international transfers, but then the game server, payment and ad features cannot be used (to refuse only personalized ads, see section 5). Transfers from the EEA and the UK rely on lawful transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Advertising partners

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Mobile Ltd. - a Unity company): ad mediation
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies Inc.): LevelPlay's default ad network
  • Google AdMob and UMP (Google LLC): ad consent form

If we add another ad network to the mediation, we will update this list before adding it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads, opting out of sale and sharing, play analytics: turn them on or off at any time in the game under [Etc → Settings → Sound and Notifications → Privacy Choices]. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalized ads are not shown until you consent.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings [Privacy → Ads]. On iOS you can turn off tracking in [Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking].
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, withdrawal of consent: contact us at the address below and we will act without delay. To confirm it is you, please include the game ID shown in the game under [Etc].
  • Users in the EEA and the UK: in addition to the rights above, you have the right to data portability (receiving your data in a machine-readable format), the right to object to processing and the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.
  • US state laws (California and others): you have the right to know what data we collect, the right to delete and correct it, and the right to opt out of the "sale or sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising (the sale and sharing opt-out in [Privacy Choices] above). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. For requests through an authorized agent and for appeals, please contact us.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or tap [Etc → Settings → Delete Data] in the game.
  • Account data on the game server (player ID, progress, leaderboards): kept until you ask for your account to be deleted, then deleted within 30 days of the request.
  • Payment records: 5 years from the transaction date (Korean E-Commerce Act).
  • Abuse records: 1 year from the date recorded (repeated abuse and forged payments: until the sanction ends).
  • Support emails: 3 years after the inquiry is closed.
  • Data kept separately by ad companies, Google, Apple and Unity follows each company's own policy.

7. Account deletion

  • In the app: [Etc → Settings → Delete Data] (two-step confirmation).
  • On the web: even after uninstalling the game, you can request deletion by email on the account deletion request page (account-deletion).

After deletion your progress and purchased items cannot be restored. Payment records that the law requires us to keep are deleted after the period in section 6.

8. Children

This game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea). In the EEA, if you are under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16), you cannot consent to personalized ads or play analytics without the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that a child's data has been collected, we delete it immediately.

9. Security

Our principles are: encrypted save files, encrypted connections to the server (HTTPS), collecting only the minimum data, and limiting server administration access to the developer alone.

10. Automatic collection technologies

The app does not use web cookies. The ad and game server SDKs store identifiers on your device (advertising ID, player ID). You can refuse them as described in section 5.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will announce it in the game and on this page 7 days before the change takes effect (30 days for changes that significantly affect your rights). Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it first.

12. Privacy Officer and Contact

  • Privacy officer: Studio Quokka
  • Email: studioquokka.support@gmail.com

You can also contact the following Korean authorities for advice on or reports of privacy violations.

  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center (Korea Internet & Security Agency): privacy.kisa.or.kr, phone 118
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: www.kopico.go.kr, +82 1833-6972

13. Language

This policy is written in Korean and provided in several translated languages. If a translation differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails. However, where the law of the country you live in gives you stronger protection, that law applies.
